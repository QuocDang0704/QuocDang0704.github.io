## Typography

Type is most of the interface. Readable type makes a design feel professional.

### Scale & hierarchy
- Use a **modular scale** (e.g. 1.2-1.25 ratio): 0.85 / 1 / 1.15 / 1.4 / 1.7 / 2.4rem.
- Create clear jumps between H1, H2, H3, and body — avoid sizes that look almost equal.
- Limit to **2 font families** (one sans for UI/body, optionally one mono for code).
- Limit weights to 2-3 (e.g. 400 / 600 / 800).

### Readability
- **Body size** 16-18px minimum for comfortable reading.
- **Line-height** ~1.5-1.65 for body, ~1.1-1.3 for large headings.
- **Measure** 60-75 characters per line for paragraphs.
- **Letter-spacing**: slightly negative on big headings (-0.01 to -0.02em), normal for body,
  slightly positive on ALL-CAPS labels.

### Polish
- Left-align body text (justified text creates rivers of whitespace).
- Use real hierarchy, not just bold everywhere.
- Consistent margins above/below headings (more space above than below).

### Review questions
- Is body text at least 16px with 1.5+ line-height?
- Are heading levels clearly distinguishable at a glance?
- Are there more than 2 font families or 3 weights in use?
